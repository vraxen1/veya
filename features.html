VEYA OS · The experience

Every interaction
has a little magic.

Thoughtful controls, expressive motion and tools that stay out of your way until you need them. This is the software vision behind VEYA.

01 · The shortcut you choose

Action Button

Placed above the power button, the Action Button gives you a quick, physical shortcut. Assign it to the action that makes sense for you: launch an app, open a tool, start a mode or trigger a favourite function.

  • Customisable actions
  • Fast access without opening menus
  • Designed as a consistent control across Number models

02 · Create as you shoot

Camera Control

One dedicated button becomes a creative control surface. Adjust the image live, choose a preset tone, or tell VEYA AI the mood you want — from a warmer look to a clean, natural finish.

  • Live image adjustments
  • Ready-made tints and tones
  • AI-assisted creative direction
  • Designed to make camera controls feel immediate

03 · There when you need it

Dynamic Island, on demand

VEYA has no permanent camera cutout or always-visible island. The front cameras and Face ID sensors are designed to sit beneath the display. Press the dedicated Dynamic Island button and a compact island appears for live activities, alerts and quick controls — then gets out of the way again.

  • No permanent display cutout
  • Under-display front cameras and Face ID sensors
  • Summoned with a dedicated physical button
  • Animated expansion and collapse

04 · Two things, one screen

Split Screen on every model

Multitasking should not be reserved for the most expensive phone. Every VEYA model is designed to support Split Screen. A small line between the apps opens a compact control panel where you can resize, swap or close a window.

  • Available across the whole VEYA lineup
  • Tap the centre line to reveal controls
  • Resize or switch app positions

05 · Ready when you are

VEYA AI

Press and hold the star on the power key to open VEYA AI in a floating, rounded-cube voice interface. Want to type instead? Tap the Type control and a text panel appears.

  • Long-press the star to start voice mode
  • Floating, rounded visual interface
  • Switch to text whenever you prefer
  • Camera Control can pass creative requests to VEYA AI

07 · Motion with meaning

Liquid-inspired animations

VEYA OS uses smooth, fluid transitions inspired by light moving through water. Panels feel connected, icons respond gently and the interface stays calm rather than overwhelming.

  • Soft transitions between screens
  • Subtle depth and layered surfaces
  • Motion that supports the action
08 · A little star, a big signal

The star is more than a logo detail. It appears in the boot experience: the star and VEYA wordmark transition as the phone starts, then the star gives way to the lock screen.
